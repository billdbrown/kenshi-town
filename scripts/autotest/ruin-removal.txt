# Does removing a ruin leave its wreckage floating, and does un-ruining it
# first avoid that? kt-test has two ruined Storm Houses: A is removed as a
# ruin, B is un-ruined (the instant repair) and then removed. Kenshi's own
# focus, frozen 1.5 s in (where it frames the house), for each pair of shots.
load kt-test
wait_ready
camera_on Storm House
wait 1.5
camera_hold
screenshot a-0-ruin
remove current
wait 4
screenshot a-1-removed-as-ruin
camera_on Storm House
wait 1.5
camera_hold
screenshot b-0-ruin
unruin current
wait 4
screenshot b-1-unruined
remove current
wait 4
screenshot b-2-removed-after-unruin
