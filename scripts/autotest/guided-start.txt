# The whole guided start, task 1 to 10, driven end to end. The founder does
# the building himself on the Engineer job, from materials in his pack and
# then from the store. Each task must wait for its real condition.
# Safe mode removes raiders: a Dust Bandit raid killed the founder 90 s in.
# (Not covered: the research itself. Sites are placed directly, which skips
# the build menu's research lock.)
load kt-test
wait_ready
expect task 1
safe on
speed 3
# His pack holds three building materials: enough for the bench.
give 3 Building Materials

# 1. Repair a Longhouse: your Town Hall
repair Longhouse
job 125 current
wait 4
expect task 1
screenshot g01-town-hall
wait_task 2 120

# 2. Set up a research bench
place_near 1875-gamedata.base 60 0
wait 4
expect task 2
screenshot g02-bench
wait_task 3 120

# 3. Research storage, build a materials store
# The store needs 1 Building Materials and 1 Iron Plates.
give 1 Building Materials
place_near 2940-gamedata.base 90 40
wait 6
expect task 3
screenshot g03-store-needs-plate
give 1 Iron Plates
wait_task 4 120
pick Storage: Building Materials
stock 20 Building Materials

# 4. Open a stone mine
place_near 1509-gamedata.base 0 130
wait 4
expect task 4
screenshot g04-mine
wait_task 5 120

# 5. Process stone into materials
# The processor needs 10 Building Materials (from the store) and 6 Iron Plates.
place_near 1863-gamedata.base -130 60
wait 6
expect task 5
screenshot g05-processor-needs-plates
give 6 Iron Plates
wait_task 6 180

# 6. Take in your first citizen
screenshot g06-citizen
arrive
wait 4
expect task 6
accept 0
wait_task 7 30

# 7. Give a citizen a job
screenshot g07-job
assign 1 Miner
people
wait_task 8 30

# 8. A bed for everyone (two people, two beds)
# A camp bed needs 1 Building Materials and a Sleeping Bag.
# (His pack takes one Sleeping Bag at a time.)
give 1 Sleeping Bag
place_near 16845-nodes_otto1.mod 50 -60
wait 15
expect task 8
screenshot g08-beds
give 1 Sleeping Bag
place_near 16845-nodes_otto1.mod 80 -60
wait_task 9 180

# 9. Stockpile 100 building materials (one store holds about 100, so a second)
screenshot g09-stockpile
pick Storage: Building Materials
stock 60 Building Materials
wait 4
expect task 9
place_near 2940-gamedata.base 140 -40 done
stock 80 Building Materials
wait_task 10 30

# 10. Ready a guard of your own
screenshot g10-guard
set_stat 2 10
wait 4
expect task 10
set_stat 2 16
wait_task 11 30
wait 2
screenshot g11-done
people
who
quit
